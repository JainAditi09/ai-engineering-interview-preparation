# Source mapping and repository rules

## Sources
- Learning syllabus: `4-Week Agentic AI Engineering Intensive.pdf` (28 days, four weekly goals, three projects, question bank and five system-design prompts).
- Interview taxonomy and company organization: https://github.com/pallavi-shekhar/ai-engineering-interview-questions-company-wise

## Organization
Follow the GitHub repository's pattern:
1. Common questions across companies, grouped by topic.
2. Company-specific questions, grouped by the same topic taxonomy.
3. Answers linked where available.
4. Company role/level and reported interview-loop context.

## Evidence labels
- `GitHub common`: question appears in the common cross-company section.
- `GitHub company-tagged`: question is listed in a company section or tagged to a company in the README.
- `PDF question`: exact question from the PDF's question bank.
- `PDF-derived practice`: an exercise created from a PDF topic, not an assertion about a real interview.
- `Role extension`: GitHub topic not directly taught in the four-week PDF.

Never invent company attribution or claim a question was asked unless the source explicitly supports it.
