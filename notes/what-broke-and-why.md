## Useful prompts
- What did I assume that turned out to be false?
- Was the issue in the model, prompt, schema, tool, retrieval, state, or infrastructure?
- How did I reproduce it?
- What guardrail or regression test prevents recurrence?
- What did the fix cost in latency, complexity, or tokens?
