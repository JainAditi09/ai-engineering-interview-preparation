## How to study each question

1. Read the explanation and rewrite the concept in your own words.
2. Draw the request/data flow where relevant.
3. Explain the trade-offs and failure modes.
4. Answer the follow-ups without looking.
5. Connect the concept to one of the projects in `../projects/`.
6. Add a test, trace, diagram, or implementation that proves your understanding.

## Source and scope

The four-week roadmap determines the learning sequence. This question bank expands the topics into explanatory interview material using general engineering knowledge. Company attribution is intentionally not invented: company-specific reports belong in `../company-wise/` and must retain their source and confidence labels.
